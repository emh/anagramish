Tutorial experiment

A: current three lessons. B: one short lesson. The same daily puzzle follows both.

Primary measure: opened a daily or practice game within 24 hours of starting the tutorial, including people who skip. Only each browser’s first recorded tutorial, with no earlier recorded game play, is eligible. The full 24 hours must pass. Results cover all experiment dates, independently of the period filter.

The 95% ranges show uncertainty, not a winning version. Plan to review after at least 100 eligible newcomers per version and 14 days; early results are descriptive. Next-day return means game activity on the next UTC day after tutorial start, once that entire day has ended.

After the tutorial

Each browser’s first tutorial attempt started in this period, with a full 24 hours since starting. Outcomes must occur within those 24 hours; game activity must follow completion of that same attempt. Daily and practice games are included. This is observational, not proof the tutorial caused a result.

Where learners stop
