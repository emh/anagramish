## Measurement corrections

The old D1 calculation admitted first plays from yesterday, before today's return day had ended. Fifteen of the reported 29 eligible players first played yesterday. The 2/29 (6.9%) figure is provisional and should not justify a retention conclusion. The corrected numerator cannot be recovered from the aggregate export alone.

- Require the entire UTC return day to end for D1 and D7, excluding early returns and nonreturns alike until their cohort matures.
- Average daily players across completed days in the selected period, excluding today and counting zero-activity days.
- Keep browsing-session traffic sources separate from new-player acquisition.
- New acquisition cohorts assign each browser to its earliest recorded game-play source, with deterministic timestamp/ID ordering. Only first plays inside the selected report period enter the cohort.
- Report first-day finishers against new players whose first UTC day has ended. A finisher completed at least one daily or practice game that day. Next-day returns require play on the next UTC calendar day, not within a rolling 24 hours.
- Returning through a different source does not change acquisition credit. Browser resets and multiple devices remain limitations.
- Rename source-level `starts` to `active_game_days`: it counted game-play session/day events, whereas summary starts correctly counts distinct game-start events. Start retries are deduplicated by session ID; day-by-day reopening creates activity but not another start.
- These changes query existing events; no migration or event backfill is required.
